No server: this page reads Arkiv directly. Survey records are shown only if their on-chain creator is the curator wallet , and they are read-only.

    (0)

      • Official records. Rows from CGWB and state groundwater reports and published studies, each re-found on its PDF page before publishing; rows whose unit is not printed on the page were left out. A curator wallet published them as read-only Arkiv entities, and this page shows only entities whose on-chain $creator is that wallet.
      • Sources and tests. JJM/NRDWP guidelines require a chemical test of every drinking-water source at least once a year, so a lab result stays current for 365 days. A field-kit reading is screening only (UDWQMP 5.1.1: kit positives go to a lab) and fades after 90 days. When no current test remains, the source shows test overdue instead of a stale "safe".
      • Who counts as a lab. The curator keeps a registry of lab wallets on Arkiv. A "lab result" from any other wallet is treated as screening.
      • Why the near-limit rule. Field kits under-read close to the limit: in published evaluations, kits read wells at 10-27 µg/L as safe (George et al. 2012) and read 21% of wells above 50 µg/L as safe (van Geen et al. 2005). Readings from half the limit upward are flagged for a lab.
      • The data contract is in arkiv/schema.md: any app can query the same entities without us.

      Limits used: arsenic 0.01 mg/L, fluoride 1.5 mg/L (IS 10500:2012). Research evidence, not household safety advice. Source code (MIT)
