Government lab results, Nadia

The Jal Jeevan Mission's own water-quality system (JJM WQMIS, Ministry of Jal Shakti) lists every lab sample from Nadia district that it flags for arsenic in FY 2025-26 (at or above the 0.01 mg/L limit). We copied that list onto Arkiv, one entity per sample, and sealed it. This page reads it back live from the chain. Nobody can now edit or delete these results before they expire, not even us, and anyone can query them without our app.
